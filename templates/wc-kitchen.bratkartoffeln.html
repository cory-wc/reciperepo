Bratkartoffeln

60m active  •  serves 4  •  615 calories  •  9g fiber
3 lb
yukon gold potatoes,
1-2" cubes
¼ C
salt
1/5 lb
bacon, thick-cut and
diced
2
onions, chopped
2 tbsp
oil
1.
Place water, salt, and potatoes in stock pot. Bring to boil, reduce heat, and boil ~10 minutes. Drain potatoes.
2.
Fry bacon until color begins to change. Add onions and fry until translucent. Remove bacon and onions from pan.
3.
Add potatoes and oil to frying pan. Cook until browned.
4.
Add bacon and onions. Cook together for 3 minutes.
