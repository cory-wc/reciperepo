# Pork Dumpling Burgers with Sesame Special Sauce

Makes 4 thick gastropub-style burgers or 6 regular burgers. Source: [Mishka Makes Food](https://mishkamakesfood.substack.com/p/pork-dumpling-burgers-w-sesame-special).

## Ingredients

### Patties

- 1½ lb (700 g) ground pork
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) light soy sauce
- 1 tsp (5 ml) sesame oil
- 1 large bunch scallions, greens finely diced
- 1-inch (3 cm) piece fresh ginger, grated
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ cup (30 g) panko breadcrumbs
- Cooking spray, as needed

### Purple cabbage slaw

- 4 cups shredded purple cabbage (½ head)
- 1 tsp (5 g) sugar
- 1 tsp (5 g) salt
- 2–4 tbsp (30–60 ml) unseasoned rice vinegar
- Sesame seeds, to taste

### Spicy sesame special sauce

- 1 cup (240 g) mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp (45 ml) Sriracha
- Zest of 1 lime, juice of ½
- 1 tsp (5 ml) sesame oil
- 2 tbsp Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)

### To serve

- 4–6 brioche burger buns, toasted

## Instructions

1. **Make the patties.** Combine pork, soy sauce, sesame oil, scallions, ginger, garlic, and panko. Mix thoroughly by hand or stand mixer until sticky and cohesive but still fluffy. Form 4 thick patties, or 6–8 smaller ones.
2. **Prepare the slaw.** Toss cabbage with salt and sugar and massage until it softens and releases liquid. Rest 5–10 minutes, then drain. Add rice vinegar to taste and sesame seeds, and toss.
3. **Make the sauce.** Mix all sauce ingredients in a medium bowl. Adjust seasoning and refrigerate until needed.
4. **Cook the patties.** Heat a skillet or grill pan over medium heat with a light coating of oil. Sear until deeply browned on one side, flip, and continue cooking. Finish thick patties in a 375°F oven.
5. **Assemble.** Toast the buns. Spread sauce on the bottom bun, then add the patty, slaw, and top bun.

## Macros per serving (1 large burger, 4 servings)

Low-calorie swaps: ground chicken for pork, light mayo for regular, and either low-carb buns or 2 pieces of butter lettuce per burger in place of the bun.
