    • Bratkartoffeln

    60m active • serves 4 • 615 calories • 9g fiber

    Ingredients

    3 lb
    yukon gold potatoes,
    1-2 inch cubes
    ¼ C
    salt
    1/5 lb
    bacon, thick-cut and
    diced
    2 whole
    onions, chopped
    2 tbsp
    oil

    Steps

    1. Place water, salt, and potatoes in stock pot. Bring to boil, reduce heat, and boil for ~10 minutes. Drain potatoes.
    2. Fry bacon until color begins to change. Add onions and fry until translucent. Remove bacon and onions from pan.
    3. Add potatoes and oil to frying pan. Cook until browned.
    4. Add bacon and onions. Cook together for 3 minutes.
